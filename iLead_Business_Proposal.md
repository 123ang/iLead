# 1 Business Proposal: iLead

**Full name:** International Lead and Enrolment Analytics Dashboard  
**System type:** Web-based international recruitment ROI dashboard  
**Institutional context:** Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM) internationalisation and student recruitment activities  
**Target users:** DVC, CIAC, Deans, programme coordinators, admission office, finance office and marketing staff  
**Recommended duration:** 6 months for research-oriented implementation; 14 weeks for MVP development  
**Estimated budget:** RM30,000 for research-grant aligned implementation

## 1.1 1. Executive Summary

iLead is a proposed web-based analytics dashboard designed to help UUM measure the return on investment of international student recruitment and marketing activities. UUM conducts international education fairs, university visits, academic collaboration trips, agency engagements and promotional campaigns. These activities involve institutional budgets, staff time and strategic expectations such as student intake, MoU/MoA, academic peers, student mobility and income generation.

The current challenge is that many international activities are documented through working papers, budgets and activity reports, but the connection between campaign spending and actual student outcomes is not consistently measured. iLead closes this gap by tracking the complete journey from campaign planning to lead capture, follow-up, application, offer, enrolment, tuition revenue and ROI.

With iLead, university leadership can identify which country, campaign, faculty, programme and activity type produces the best recruitment results. The system supports evidence-based decision-making, better budget allocation and stronger accountability for internationalisation activities.

## 1.2 2. Background and Rationale

UUM has ongoing internationalisation initiatives involving overseas recruitment and institutional collaboration. Existing proposal papers normally include the purpose of the visit, proposed delegation, programme schedule, expected KPIs and estimated budget. However, the post-event outcome tracking is usually fragmented across lead lists, staff follow-ups, application records and financial claims.

The university therefore needs a centralised dashboard that can link activity-based reporting with outcome-based measurement. This will allow management to move from asking “What activities did we conduct?” to “Which activities generated students, revenue and strategic value?”

## 1.3 3. Problem Statement

At present, international recruitment and collaboration activities may be approved with clear targets, but the results are not always tracked in a closed-loop manner. There is no single system that combines campaign budget, student leads, staff follow-up, application status, offer status, enrolment status and tuition revenue.

Because of this gap, UUM may not be able to easily answer the following strategic questions:

- How many student leads were generated from each campaign?
- How many leads were contacted, interested, applied, offered and enrolled?
- Which country produces the highest conversion rate?
- Which faculty and programme generate the best recruitment return?
- How much does it cost to recruit one enrolled international student?
- Which international activities should receive more budget in the future?
- Which campaigns should be improved, reduced or discontinued?

## 1.4 4. Proposed Solution: iLead

iLead stands for **International Lead and Enrolment Analytics Dashboard**. It is a centralised system that records every recruitment campaign, captures leads during international activities, tracks staff follow-up, matches leads with application and enrolment records, and calculates recruitment ROI automatically.

The system is designed around one main flow:

> Campaign -\> Lead -\> Follow-up -\> Application -\> Offer -\> Enrolment -\> Revenue -\> ROI

## 1.5 5. Business Objectives

1.  Create a centralised system for recording all international recruitment campaigns and collaboration activities.
2.  Track prospective students from first contact until final enrolment.
3.  Measure the financial and recruitment return of each campaign.
4.  Identify the best-performing countries, faculties, programmes and campaign types.
5.  Reduce manual reporting and provide real-time management visibility.
6.  Support UUM internationalisation KPIs such as student intake, MoU/MoA, mobility, academic peers and education programme income.
7.  Provide a data governance framework for sustainable recruitment performance monitoring.

## 1.6 6. Scope of the System

| Module                   | Purpose                                                                                                            |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Campaign Management      | Register campaigns, countries, dates, budget, objectives, target KPIs and responsible staff.                       |
| Lead Capture             | Collect prospective student details using mobile-friendly forms or Excel/CSV upload.                               |

| Governance and Audit Log | Track changes, user actions and data quality responsibilities.                                                     |

## 1.8 8. Business Process Flow

1.  Campaign proposal is prepared and approved with target KPIs and budget.
2.  Campaign is registered in iLead before travel or event execution.
3.  Staff conduct international marketing activities such as fairs, university visits, roadshows or partner meetings.
4.  Student leads are captured using a mobile form or uploaded from Excel/CSV.
5.  Leads are assigned to responsible staff for follow-up.
6.  Staff update follow-up status and next action dates.
7.  Student submits application to UUM.
8.  Registrar/admission data is uploaded or integrated into iLead.
9.  System matches leads with application, offer and enrolment records.
10. Finance data is uploaded to confirm campaign spending.
11. System calculates KPIs, conversion rates and ROI.
12. Management reviews dashboard and decides whether to continue, improve or stop similar campaigns.

## 1.9 9. Metrics to be Measured

### 1.9.1 9.1 Recruitment Metrics

| Metric                    | Formula / Measurement                                | Purpose                                           |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Total Leads               | Count of all captured leads                          | Measures campaign reach.                          |
| Qualified Leads           | Count of hot and warm leads                          | Measures lead quality.                            |
| Total Applications        | Count of leads/applicants who submitted applications | Measures application generation.                  |
| Total Offers              | Count of issued offer letters                        | Measures admission success.                       |
| Total Enrolments          | Count of registered students                         | Measures final recruitment outcome.               |
| Lead-to-Application Rate  | Applications / Leads x 100                           | Measures conversion from interest to application. |
| Application-to-Offer Rate | Offers / Applications x 100                          | Measures admission conversion.                    |
| Offer-to-Enrolment Rate   | Enrolments / Offers x 100                            | Measures offer acceptance.                        |
| Overall Conversion Rate   | Enrolments / Leads x 100                             | Measures end-to-end campaign effectiveness.       |

### 1.9.2 9.2 Financial Metrics

| Metric                    | Formula / Measurement                      | Purpose                                           |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Campaign Spend            | Approved budget or actual spending         | Measures total campaign investment.               |
| Cost per Lead             | Campaign Spend / Total Leads               | Measures acquisition cost per lead.               |
| Cost per Application      | Campaign Spend / Applications              | Measures cost to generate an application.         |
| Cost per Offer            | Campaign Spend / Offers                    | Measures cost to generate an offer.               |
| Cost per Enrolled Student | Campaign Spend / Enrolments                | Measures cost to recruit one actual student.      |
| Estimated Tuition Revenue | Enrolments x Tuition Fee                   | Estimates direct revenue from recruited students. |
| Net Return                | Estimated Tuition Revenue - Campaign Spend | Measures financial value after cost.              |
| ROI Ratio                 | Estimated Tuition Revenue / Campaign Spend | Shows return multiple.                            |
| ROI Percentage            | Net Return / Campaign Spend x 100          | Shows profitability percentage.                   |

### 1.9.3 9.3 Operational and Strategic Metrics

| Metric                     | Purpose                                                           |
|----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Average Follow-up Time     | Measures how quickly staff respond to leads.                      |
| Overdue Follow-ups         | Identifies leads that need urgent attention.                      |
| Lead Drop-off Stage        | Shows where students stop progressing.                            |
| Country Conversion Rate    | Identifies high-performing countries.                             |
| Faculty Conversion Rate    | Compares faculty recruitment performance.                         |
| Programme Conversion Rate  | Identifies high-demand programmes.                                |
| MoU/MoA Count              | Measures partnership outcomes linked to campaigns.                |
| Student Mobility Count     | Tracks inbound or outbound mobility outcomes.                     |
| Academic Peer Count        | Supports ranking-related academic reputation tracking.            |

## 1.11 11. Example ROI Calculation

| Item                              | Example Value |
|-----------------------------------|---------------|

| Offers                            | 20            |

| Metric                    | Calculation          | Result     |
|---------------------------|----------------------|------------|
| Cost per Lead             | RM21,245 / 100       | RM212.45   |
| Lead-to-Application Rate  | 30 / 100 x 100       | 30%        |
| Application-to-Offer Rate | 20 / 30 x 100        | 66.7%      |
| Offer-to-Enrolment Rate   | 10 / 20 x 100        | 50%        |
| Overall Conversion Rate   | 10 / 100 x 100       | 10%        |
| Cost per Enrolled Student | RM21,245 / 10        | RM2,124.50 |
| ROI Ratio                 | RM200,000 / RM21,245 | 9.41x      |
| Net Return                | RM200,000 - RM21,245 | RM178,755  |

## 1.14 14. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk                                    | Possible Impact                              | Mitigation Strategy                                               |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Incomplete lead entry                   | Campaign result may be underreported.        | Use simple mobile form and make lead entry part of campaign SOP.  |
| Poor follow-up discipline               | Good leads may be lost.                      | Use assignment, reminders and overdue follow-up alerts.           |
| Difficulty matching leads to applicants | ROI may be inaccurate.                       | Use email, phone, passport, source campaign and programme fields. |
| Delayed SIS integration                 | Dashboard may depend on manual upload first. | Start with CSV upload and plan SIS integration as phase 2.        |
| Data privacy concerns                   | Risk to student personal data.               | Apply role-based access, audit logs, HTTPS and data minimisation. |
| Low user adoption                       | System may not be used consistently.         | Provide training, management mandate and clear ownership.         |

## 1.18 18. Conclusion

iLead provides a practical and strategic solution for tracking UUM’s international recruitment outcomes. It connects campaign spending with real student recruitment results and helps UUM move from activity-based reporting to evidence-based performance management.
